About ToletEarth

ToletEarth was born from a simple idea: finding a home shouldn't cost a fortune. No brokers. No hidden fees. Just real people connecting directly.

Our Journey So Far

What started as a small idea has grown into something real — 12 verified properties across Chandigarh, Mohali, and Panchkula. We didn't just wait for listings to come to us. We went flat to flat, door to door, talking to owners, verifying homes, and building a platform we can be proud of.

We believe in trust, transparency, and community. Every listing you see on ToletEarth has been personally checked or verified by us — so you can search with confidence.

What We Offer

🏠 For Homeowners

List your property absolutely free. No brokerage. No hidden charges. Just connect with genuine tenants directly.

🔍 For Renters

Browse real properties with clear details, photos, and direct owner contact. Save thousands in brokerage and find a home that truly fits your needs.

💬 For Everyone

We're here to make renting fair, simple, and accessible for everyone in the Tricity area.
