About TOLET Earth

TOLET Earth was born from a simple idea: finding a home shouldn't cost a fortune. No brokers. No hidden fees. Just real people connecting directly.

Our Journey So Far

What started as a small idea has grown into something real — 12 verified properties across Chandigarh, Mohali, and Panchkula. We didn't just wait for listings to come to us. We went flat to flat, door to door, talking to owners, verifying homes, and building a platform we can be proud of.

We believe in trust, transparency, and community. Every listing you see on TOLET Earth has been personally checked and verified by us — so you can search with confidence.

Meet the Founder

Aman — a dreamer, a doer, and someone who believes that housing should be simple. From knocking on doors to building this platform, every step has been driven by one goal: to help people find a place they can call home.
