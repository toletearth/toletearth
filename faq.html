Frequently Asked Questions

Is TOLET Earth completely free?
Yes! TOLET Earth is 100% free for both homeowners and renters. We never charge brokerage, listing fees, or any hidden costs. Our mission is to make renting fair and accessible for everyone.
How do I list my property?
Click on the "List Your Property" button on our homepage. Fill in the details about your property (location, rent, photos, etc.) and submit. We'll review and publish it as soon as possible.
How can I trust the listings on TOLET Earth?
We personally verify every listing on our platform. We visit properties, talk to owners, and confirm details before publishing. This helps ensure that renters find genuine, reliable homes.
Do you operate in my city?
Currently, we serve Chandigarh, Mohali, and Panchkula. We're expanding soon! If you'd like to see TOLET Earth in your city, drop us an email at info@toletearth.com.
How do I contact the owner of a property?
Each listing shows the owner's phone number directly. If the owner has chosen privacy mode, you can contact us and we'll connect you.
